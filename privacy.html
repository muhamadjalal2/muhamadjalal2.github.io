Nova Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Muhamad Jalal ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Nova Rush mobile application ("the App").

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from the authentication provider.
  • Game Data: Game progress, achievements, scores, and in-game purchases are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to the cloud.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Usage Data: Analytics data such as features used, session duration, and interaction patterns to improve the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's features
  • Save your game progress and sync across devices
  • Improve the App and develop new features
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud storage
  • Google Sign-In: Account authentication
  • Facebook Login: Account authentication
  • Google AdMob: Advertising and ad performance measurement
  • Google Play Services: Play Integrity and app services

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing the use of your data.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Firestore and encrypted local storage. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of analytics collection

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: muhamadjalal.mobdev@gmail.com
